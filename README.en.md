# 📋 Social Survey Management System

[🇧🇷 Português](README.md) | 🇺🇸 English | [🇪🇸 Español](README.es.md)

> A waiting-list management system started in 2025 from a real operational need. It integrates Google Forms, Google Sheets, and Google Apps Script to organize registrations, analyze unmet demand, and support data-informed management.

## 💼 Project origin

The project began in **2025** after identifying **unmet demand** and the need to improve the organization and follow-up of people waiting for places in activities and services.

The need was first discussed during a **management committee meeting**. The solution was then planned jointly with **pedagogical coordination**, while workflow rules and requirements were developed with the collaboration of **two social work professionals**.

Transforming a predominantly manual process into a structured digital workflow also motivated deeper study of **automation, Google Workspace, and Google Apps Script**.

The project therefore combines:

- a response to a real management and organizational need;
- a practical laboratory for automation development and learning.

To preserve privacy, the repository uses a generic approach and does not identify the original organization.

## 🎯 Objective

Digitize and organize the registration of people waiting for available places, reducing manual work, standardizing information, and supporting demand analysis and opportunity management.

The system does not replace formal admission or enrollment procedures. Its role is to organize demand and support the responsible team.
